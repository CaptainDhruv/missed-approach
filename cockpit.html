Primary flight display Electric AHRS and air data computer

Speed tape left, altitude and vertical speed right, heading at the bottom. Cyan marks your selections. The PFD needs battery and avionics power, but its attitude comes from an electric AHRS, so it keeps working when the vacuum pump fails.

Airspeed
Pitot minus static pressure, in knots. White arc for flaps, green normal, yellow caution, red line never exceed.
Attitude
Vacuum gyro. Pitch and bank against the horizon. Tumbles slowly if suction is lost.
Altimeter
29.92
Static pressure altitude in feet. Set the local altimeter: ATIS reports 30.01 in Hg.
Turn coordinator
Electric gyro. Wing on the mark is a standard-rate turn, 3° per second. Step on the ball to centre it.
Heading
090°
Vacuum gyro heading. The orange bug is the autopilot heading. SYNC realigns the card to the compass.
Vertical speed
Climb or descent in feet per minute, from the rate of static pressure change. Lags a few seconds.
